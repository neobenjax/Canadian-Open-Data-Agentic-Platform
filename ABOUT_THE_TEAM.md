## 🤝 Collaborative Philosophy & Review Loop

- **Collective Full-Stack AI Literacy:** We reject traditional corporate silos where one person only writes code and others only write documents. Everyone writes agent code, tests evals, configures tools, and opens PRs.
- **Rotating Project Leadership:** CanData-MCP is our first launch. As we progress, each member takes the Team Lead role on subsequent collaborative projects so everyone highlights both technical depth and Team Leadership on their resumes.
- **Triangular Peer Review Loop:** To ensure thorough code quality with a 3-person core:
  - **Benjamin's PRs** $\rightarrow$ Reviewed and approved by **Samir**
  - **Samir's PRs** $\rightarrow$ Reviewed and approved by **Yassir**
  - **Yassir's PRs** $\rightarrow$ Reviewed and approved by **Benjamin**
- **Mentorship Anchors:** Each member serves as the friendly domain guide for their superpower, helping teammates cross-train in FinTech, Data Visualization, Delivery, and Security.
