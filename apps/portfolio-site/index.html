The Challenge

The Open Data Breakdown in Canada

Federal and provincial portals host thousands of valuable tables, but structural barriers prevent actionable analysis.

Fragmented Portals & Schemas

Canadian open data is scattered across incompatible CKAN catalogs, provincial silos, and inconsistent CSV formats without unified data dictionaries.

Siloed Data Infrastructure

The Verification Gap

General-purpose chatbots hallucinate numerical values and fabricate citations. Decision-makers cannot risk relying on unverifiable statistical claims.

Lack of Provenance

Wasted Civic Research Hours

Data journalists and civic researchers spend over 80% of project time downloading, formatting, and stitching datasets together rather than synthesizing findings.
