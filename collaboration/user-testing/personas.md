# Personas for the simulated pilot

All three personas are fictional. They use no real names or employers.

Round 1 used the numbered questions below. Round 2 added follow-up and gap-closing questions
for the same personas; see `pilot_results.md`.

## Analyst A: municipal housing policy analyst

- **Background:** works in a city housing department; reads CPI and housing tables daily.
- **Goal:** know whether shelter costs are rising faster than overall prices in Toronto.
- **Cares about:** the right period, the right city, an answer she can put in a briefing note.

| # | Question (own words) | Baseline |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | In August 2026, how did shelter prices in Toronto change compared with overall prices over the past year? | GT-12: shelter -0.47%, all-items +2.14% (CAN-32 run); benchmark -0.5% and +2.1% |
| 5 | How many housing units were started in Abbotsford in 2025? | None. Probes the pause-and-ask step (duplicate place names) |

## Economist B: university labour and housing economist

- **Background:** academic researcher; checks every figure's source and quality flag.
- **Goal:** get exact figures with citations she can verify.
- **Cares about:** series IDs, publishing agency, data-quality flags, revision dates.

| # | Question (own words) | Baseline |
|---|---|---|
| 2 | How many homes were started in the Toronto area in 2025, and how is that different from 2024? | GT-05: 26,087 (2025) vs 37,718 (2024), -30.8% |
| 6 | What is the latest year-over-year change in the consumer price index for Vancouver? | None. Tests discovery on a series not in the benchmark |

## Journalist C: newsroom reporter, no statistics background

- **Background:** writes for a general audience; needs a figure she can quote safely.
- **Goal:** a plain answer, and a clear message when the data cannot answer.
- **Cares about:** readable wording, not being misled, knowing when to stop.

| # | Question (own words) | Baseline |
|---|---|---|
| 3 | How many times did the Bank of Canada change interest rates in 2025, and where did the rate start and end the year? | GT-13: 4 changes, 3.25% to 2.25% (series V39079) |
| 4 | What will the average rent in Toronto be in 2030? | Expected refusal (forecast). Not comparable by number |
