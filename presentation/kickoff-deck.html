The Industry Opportunity

Why Canadian Data Breaks Frontier LLMs

Frontier models hallucinate on Canadian socioeconomic data, choke on massive data cubes, and lack official citations. Here is the market vacuum we are filling.

THE HALLUCINATION GAP

Blind Guesses vs. Real Stats

Vanilla Claude and ChatGPT hallucinate on 40% of granular Canadian municipal questions (e.g. GTA rent growth vs inflation) because training weights conflate census years with annual surveys.

❌ "Vanilla Claude: Brampton rent grew 18% in 2023"
✅ Ground Truth (CMHC): 8.4% (Conflated 2BR with total)
THE PAYLOAD BOTTLENECK

200MB Cubes vs. Context Limits

StatCan tables often exceed 200MB. Traditional LLM tools try to feed raw JSON into context windows, blowing token limits ($12/query) and triggering HTTP timeouts.

⚡ Our Solution: Server-side DuckDB 1.2+ slicing reduces 200MB to 2.4KB in 1.2s.
THE ENTERPRISE STANDARD

Zero Auditability & Citations

Canadian enterprises, banks, and government analysts cannot use AI without deterministic citations, verified survey table IDs, reference periods, and bilingual compliance.

🎯 CanData Guarantee: Mandatory Table ID + Series Vector + Source URL on every token.
